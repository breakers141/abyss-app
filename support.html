What Abyss does

Abyss is a Safari extension that gives websites a comfortable dark appearance. It tries to keep each site looking like itself, with readable text, natural photos and controls that keep working. Choose when it darkens and a theme once, then browse as normal.

Under Darken websites in the app:

  • Automatic: darkens websites when your device is in dark mode.
  • Always: darkens websites at all times.
  • Off: never darkens websites.

And four sea themes:

  • Twilight Zone: a soft blue-grey, where the last light fades.
  • Midnight Zone: a balanced, warm charcoal dark.
  • Abyssal Plain: true black, as deep as it goes.
  • Lantern: a warm amber glow, your own light in the deep.

You can also give one website its own theme and its own Automatic, Always or Off, and choose whether bright photographs are gently dimmed (Normal or Slightly Dimmed in the app). Websites that already have a good dark mode can keep using it.

Abyss works on your device. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Turn on Abyss on iPhone and iPad

Open the Abyss app first. It walks you through three short setup steps, and you can find them again any time under Safari Setup Guide in the app.

  1. Open Abyss's Safari settings If the app shows an Open Safari Extension Settings button, use it. Or open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Abyss. On iOS 17 and iPadOS 17, open Settings → Safari → Extensions → Abyss.
  2. Turn on Allow Extension This lets Safari load Abyss while you browse.
  3. Allow All Websites Under Permissions, set Website Access for All Websites to Allow. If Safari shows Other Websites with Ask, Allow and Deny, choose Allow.

If you use Safari profiles, turn on Abyss for each profile you want to use it with.

Using Abyss in Safari

Open a website, then open Safari's page menu next to the address bar (on iOS 17 this is the aA button) and choose Abyss. On iPad, Safari may also show an extensions button in its toolbar. The Abyss panel lets you:

  • set Darken this website to Automatic, Always or Off (Default follows what you chose in the app),
  • choose a Theme for this website (Default uses the theme you chose in the app),
  • choose whether to Use the website's own dark mode,
  • and Reset website settings to go back to your usual choices.

Turn on Abyss on Mac

Open the Abyss app on your Mac. In General, set Darken websites to Automatic or Always and choose a theme. The app's Safari Setup section shows these steps too.

  1. Open Safari settings In Safari, choose Safari → Settings → Extensions. The app's Open Safari Extension Settings button can take you there.
  2. Turn on Abyss Select Abyss and enable its extension. Safari may ask you to confirm.
  3. Allow website access Open Abyss's website access controls (Edit Websites when shown) and allow it on all websites. If Safari asks when you first click Abyss's toolbar button, allow it on every website.
  4. Check a webpage Open a page in Safari. Use Abyss's toolbar controls to choose an appearance or disable it for that website.

If you use Safari profiles, enable Abyss in each profile where you want it.

Common questions

A website looks wrong

Most websites were designed for a light background, and a few do not darken cleanly. You can:

  • open the Abyss panel on that website and try a different Theme,
  • set Darken this website to Off, so the site goes back to its original look,
  • reload the page if a change does not show straight away.

Abyss does not recolour videos, or pictures a website draws itself (canvas and WebGL, which some maps, charts and games use).

Please tell us about it at abyss.support@icloud.com. It helps to include the website address, your device and its system version, and a screenshot if you can. Abyss never sends this for you; you choose what to share.

A website already has its own dark mode

When Use the website's own dark mode is on (it is on unless you change it), Abyss is designed to leave a good dark theme a website already shows in control. It does not switch the website's own theme on for you: use the website's own setting for that, or your device's dark appearance if the website follows it.

If you prefer Abyss's look on that website, turn Use the website's own dark mode off in the Abyss panel and Abyss will apply its own dark appearance.

Turning Abyss off for one website

On that website, open the Abyss panel and set Darken this website to Off. Set it back to Default the same way. You can also add a website in the Abyss app, under Manage Websites on iPhone and iPad or Websites on Mac, and use Reset website settings to go back to your usual choices.

To pause Abyss everywhere, set Darken websites to Off in the Abyss app.

Why does Abyss ask for website access?

Safari asks for permission for Abyss to read and modify webpages. This permission is necessary to calculate and apply a dark appearance. Page processing happens on your device. Abyss does not use that access to build a browsing profile, collect form data, or sell information.

The privacy policy explains exactly what Abyss reads, including when it downloads a website's own stylesheet or picture again to read its colours.

What data does Abyss collect?

None. Abyss does not collect personal information or browsing data. It does not send the websites you visit, page contents, search queries, form entries, account information, or browsing history to us or to third parties. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your appearance choices and website settings are stored on your device. Details are in the privacy policy.

Abyss does not seem to work

  • Check that Allow Extension is on and Website Access for All Websites is set to Allow (the steps above). Safari does not tell the Abyss app whether website access is allowed, so please check that in Safari's settings.
  • Check that Darken websites is not set to Off in the Abyss app. With Automatic, Abyss darkens websites only while your device is in dark mode.
  • If you use Safari profiles, turn on Abyss in the profile you are using.
  • Private Browsing has its own Safari permission. Turn it on in Abyss's Safari settings if you want Abyss in private tabs.
  • Abyss works on websites. It cannot change Safari's own pages, such as the Start Page.

Do my settings sync between devices?

No. Abyss keeps your appearance and website settings on each device and does not sync them. Safari may sync whether the extension is installed and turned on, depending on your Apple settings; that is managed by Apple.
