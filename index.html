The same page, light and in Midnight Zone colours

Dive

Every website, darkened with care.

Abyss is a Safari extension. It reads each page's own colours and makes a dark version of them, so websites keep looking like themselves, with readable text, natural photos and controls that keep working.

  • Automatic or Always

    Darken websites when your device is in dark mode, or at all times. Off is there too, whenever you want the original look back.

  • Photos stay natural

    Photos and videos keep their original colours unless you choose Slightly Dimmed. Dark logos and icons can be shown inverted, so they stay visible.

  • Website by website

    From the Abyss button in Safari, give any website its own theme and its own Automatic, Always or Off. Reset it whenever you like.

  • Good dark modes stay

    When a website already shows a good dark theme of its own, Abyss is designed to leave it in charge.

  • The small windows too

    Cookie windows, sign-in, payment and human-check boxes inside a page are darkened along with it. What you type in them is never read.

  • Private on your device

    Pages are processed on your device. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What happens on your device stays there.

Abyss does not collect personal information or browsing data. It does not send the websites you visit, page contents, search queries, form entries, account information, or browsing history to us or to third parties.

To darken a page, Abyss may download that site's own stylesheet and picture files again, without cookies. Nothing is sent anywhere else. The privacy policy explains exactly what it reads.

Questions

Where does Abyss work?

Abyss is a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It works on websites in Safari. It cannot change Safari's own pages, such as the Start Page.

Does every website darken cleanly?

Most websites were designed for a light background, and a few do not darken cleanly. On that website, open the Abyss panel and try a different theme, or set Darken this website to Off to go back to the original look.

Abyss does not recolour videos, or pictures a website draws itself (canvas and WebGL, which some maps, charts and games use).

What if a website already has its own dark mode?

When Use the website's own dark mode is on (it is on unless you change it), Abyss is designed to leave a good dark theme a website already shows in control. If you prefer Abyss's look there, turn it off in the Abyss panel.

Why does Safari ask for website access?

Safari asks for permission for Abyss to read and modify webpages. This permission is necessary to calculate and apply a dark appearance. Page processing happens on your device. Abyss does not use that access to build a browsing profile, collect form data, or sell information.

What data does Abyss collect?

None. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your appearance choices and website settings are stored on your device. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do my settings sync between devices?

No. Abyss keeps your appearance and website settings on each device and does not sync them.
